Research Associates

Support research planning, literature reviews, research methodology development, and academic documentation.

Support research planning, literature reviews, research methodology development, and academic documentation.

As a Research Associate at Proofix, you will work alongside research professionals and subject matter experts to support scholars in developing high-quality academic and technical work.

This role suits postgraduate students, research scholars, PhD holders and fresh graduates with strong technical skills and a willingness to learn.

Key responsibilities

  • Support research planning.
  • Assist with literature reviews.
  • Contribute to research methodology development.
  • Prepare and support academic documentation.

Preferred skills

Research PlanningLiterature ReviewResearch MethodologyAcademic DocumentationAcademic Writing

Who can apply?

  • Postgraduate students and research scholars with relevant technical expertise.
  • PhD holders and experienced research professionals.
  • Freelance researchers.
  • Fresh graduates with strong technical skills and a willingness to learn.

Relevant qualifications, practical skills, research experience, and a commitment to quality are valued. We are committed to responsible research support and do not encourage plagiarism, fabricated results, or misrepresentation of academic authorship.
