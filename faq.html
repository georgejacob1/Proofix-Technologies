Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear, transparent answers about our academic research support services, methodology consultations, ethical standards, and collaboration timelines.

Core Research Support Services

1. What research support services do you provide?
We provide comprehensive research support for PhD scholars, postgraduate students, and researchers, including research proposal and synopsis preparation, literature review, research methodology, data analysis, thesis and dissertation support, research paper writing, manuscript preparation, and publication guidance.
2. Can you help me choose or refine my research topic?
Yes. We can help you evaluate your research idea, identify research gaps, refine the research problem, formulate objectives and research questions, and develop a clear and researchable topic aligned with your academic domain.
3. Do you provide support with statistical analysis and interpretation?
Yes. We provide support for data preparation, statistical analysis, selection of appropriate tests, result interpretation, tables and graphical presentation, and discussion of findings using suitable statistical methodologies and software (such as Python, R, SPSS, AMOS, Stata, and MATLAB).
4. Can you help with thesis and dissertation work?
Yes. We provide structured support across different stages of thesis and dissertation development, including chapter planning, literature review, methodology, analysis, results, discussion, formatting, referencing, proofreading, and overall academic presentation.
5. Do you provide research publication support?
Yes. We assist researchers with manuscript preparation, journal selection, formatting, submission preparation, reviewer-response support, and publication strategy. Our focus is on helping researchers develop clear, rigorous, and academically sound research suitable for indexed journals (Scopus, SCI, Web of Science).

Ethics, Confidentiality & Delivery

6. How does Proofix ensure academic integrity and originality?
Proofix operates strictly under ethical academic guidance frameworks. All research outputs undergo rigorous plagiarism and originality audits using institutional scanning software. We empower scholars by refining their foundational thinking and providing reproducible methodological steps, never compromising academic honesty.
7. How do revisions and supervisor feedback get addressed?
We consider supervisor and reviewer feedback a natural, constructive phase of academic progression. Phase 6 of our methodology is explicitly dedicated to feedback integration: our advisors review supervisor remarks with you and refine the specific sections until requirements are satisfied.
8. What is the typical turnaround timeline for a research milestone?
Timelines depend on the complexity of the project. Research proposal support typically takes 7 to 14 days, statistical data analysis takes 10 to 18 days, while comprehensive thesis chapter guidance is milestone-scheduled over weeks. We establish clear delivery milestones during Phase 3 (Research Planning).
