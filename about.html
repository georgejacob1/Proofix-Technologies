About Proofix Technologies

Research is Remembered for its Impact, Not its Length.

Proofix Technologies Pvt Ltd is dedicated to providing professional academic research assistance and technical support for scholars and researchers facing complex research challenges.

We understand that modern research demands a synthesis of deep academic knowledge, technical acumen, data analysis capability, methodological rigor, and lucid scientific communication.

Leadership Perspective

The Story Behind Proofix Technologies

“My journey into founding Proofix began when I completed my doctoral studies. The path was challenging, filled with uncertainty, and I often wished for a mentor who truly understood the complexities of the doctoral journey—not just academically, but methodologically.”

“After spending years evaluating research papers and advising scholars across disciplines, I witnessed countless brilliant minds struggle with translating their ideas into structured academic papers. In 2015, Proofix was founded with a simple mission: to be the guide I wished I had during my own doctoral pursuit.”

“Today, our multidisciplinary team brings together domain experts from Engineering, AI, Business, and Health Sciences to assist researchers worldwide with uncompromising academic standards.”

Evolution & Growth

Our Journey of Excellence

How Proofix evolved into a trusted research support organization for scholars globally.

2015

Foundation & Vision

Proofix was established with a focus on democratizing access to expert PhD research guidance, starting with an initial cohort of 12 doctoral candidates.

2017

Multidisciplinary Expansion

Expanded our mentor network to include advisors across Engineering, AI, and Social Sciences. Launched our comprehensive research proposal mentorship program.

2019

Peer Recognition & Quality Standard

Achieved a 95% proposal approval rate across partner university candidates. Recognized by academic mentoring forums for ethical and structured guidance.

2021

Digital Innovation & Global Consulting

Launched specialized technical support for Machine Learning, Big Data analytics, and virtual consultation suites for international scholars.

2023

500+ Milestone Achievement

Celebrated mentoring our 500th doctoral scholar to proposal defense and indexed journal acceptance (Scopus/SCI).

2026

Continued Excellence & Global Impact

Now guiding researchers across 45+ universities globally with an industry-leading 98% client satisfaction rate.
