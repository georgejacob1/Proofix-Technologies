Who Can Apply?

We encourage applications from:

  • Postgraduate students and research scholars with relevant technical expertise.
  • PhD holders and experienced research professionals.
  • Digital marketing professionals and lead generation specialists.
  • Business development executives and experienced managers.
  • Freelance researchers and subject matter experts.
  • Academic editors and statistical consultants.
  • Fresh graduates with strong technical skills and a willingness to learn.

Relevant qualifications, practical skills, research experience, and a commitment to quality are valued.

Our Work Culture

At Proofix, we value:

  • Research integrity and academic ethics.
  • Quality, accuracy, and attention to detail.
  • Collaboration and knowledge sharing.
  • Professional responsibility and transparency.
  • Continuous improvement and innovation.

We are committed to responsible research support and do not encourage plagiarism, fabricated results, or misrepresentation of academic authorship.
