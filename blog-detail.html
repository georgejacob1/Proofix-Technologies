How to Write a Quality Research Proposal?

Research Proposal3 min read

Writing a quality research proposal is utmost important to start with your PhD journey. By quality proposal, the emphasis should be on the uniqueness of the research which is being presented.

Various attributes which are to be focused while preparing a research proposal are depicted below:

  1. Start with a novel problem statement that exists in the selected area of research.
  2. Formulate a novel topic/title of your research with the focus on how you are going to overcome the identified problem from existing research.
  3. Define a clear aim of your research which focus on the solution you expect to achieve through your research.
  4. Formulate a minimum of four research objectives which shall guide you to accomplish your research concept.
  5. A well defined and novel research methodology to be framed which makes sure that you have a useful and concrete contribution towards the topic which is selected.

To achieve this, constant self upgradation on the trends and advancements of each research area is necessary. Our team of experts helps in devising unique problem identification, impactful research ideas and possible findings to make your research a novel one.
